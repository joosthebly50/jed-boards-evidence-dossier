## 1. Administrative Striking Off of `JED BOARDS PTE. LTD.` (`UEN: 201801893M`) Despite Unpaid Creditor Liabilities

Official ACRA records obtained on **9 November 2026** ([`Exhibit 72`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_72_2026-11-09_ACRA_Business_Profile_JED_BOARDS_PTE_LTD_UEN_201801893M_Struck_Off.pdf), verifiable at `https://www.acratrustbar.gov.sg/verify/AHZk-WnXq5`) confirm that **`JED BOARDS PTE. LTD.`** (`UEN 201801893M`, formerly `ELECTRIC SKATEBOARDS PTE. LTD.`, incorporated `15 January 2018` with **`SGD 400,000.00` in paid-up share capital** and **`SGD 2,000,000.00` in issued share capital**) was administratively **`STRUCK OFF`** on **8 February 2021**—not wound up through formal insolvency liquidation, where a liquidator or Official Receiver would examine the company's bank accounts and unpaid creditor claims.

Under **Section 344 of the Companies Act** and ACRA's applicable striking-off requirements, a company seeking to be struck off must have **no outstanding liabilities or unresolved creditor claims**.

However, documented evidence proves that at the exact time the striking-off application was pending before ACRA, the company had substantial unpaid customer liabilities across multiple jurisdictions and was actively assuring creditors that bank refunds were being processed:

- On **11 January 2021**—just **28 days** before `JED BOARDS PTE. LTD.` was officially struck off on **8 February 2021**—the company emailed me ([`Exhibit 18`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_18_2021-01-07_to_2021-01-11_Jed_Boards_Email_Transaction_Too_Old_Requesting_IBAN_and_Joost_Reply.png)) stating:
  > *"Sorry for the late reply, our team has been off during Christmas and New Years. Our accounts team has just updated us and said because the transaction is so old we can't refund it back to your card, it will need to go back to a bank account. Can you please provide us a bank account that we can process the refund back to."*
- I immediately provided my IBAN bank account details (`NL87 INGB 0005 2589 58`) on **11 January 2021** for my unfulfilled May 2018 order of **`USD 2,224.00`** (`Orders JED-1219-17` and `JED-1228-17`, [`Exhibit 01`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_01_2018-05_to_2019-07_Joost_Hebly_Order_Confirmations_and_Email_Overview.docx), [`Exhibit 02`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_02_2018-06_ICS_Credit_Card_Statement_EUR_1705_28_Order_JED-1228-17.pdf), [`Exhibit 07`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_07_2019-07-10_Joost_Hebly_Formal_Cancellation_and_Full_Refund_Demand_USD_2224.png), [`Exhibit 08`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_08_2019-07-10_Support_Ticket_1789_Cancel_Order_1228-17_and_1219-17_Full_Refund.png), [`Exhibit 17`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_17_2019-12-28_to_2020-01-06_Jed_Boards_Email_Expired_Card_Manual_Refund_Excuse.png), [`Exhibit 18`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_18_2021-01-07_to_2021-01-11_Jed_Boards_Email_Transaction_Too_Old_Requesting_IBAN_and_Joost_Reply.png)). I was never notified of the pending ACRA striking-off application, depriving me of the statutory opportunity to lodge a creditor's objection under Section 344, and no refund was ever paid.
- Multiple other international customers were left with unfulfilled orders and unpaid refunds under identical *"accounts team / expired card"* excuses, including Australian resident **James Rose** (`AUD 2,900.84` / `USD 2,344.00`, [`Exhibit 31`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_31_2019-05-06_Victim_James_Rose_Instagram_DM_Order_JED-1184-17_Paid_Unfulfilled_AUD_2900_84.png), [`Exhibit 35`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_35_2019-07-31_Victim_James_Rose_Jed_Boards_Email_Confirming_Original_Card_Active_for_Refund.png), [`Exhibit 38`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_38_2019-08_Victim_James_Rose_esk8_Builders_Forum_Post_Order_JED-1184-17_USD_2344_Missing_Refund.png)), **Raul** (`USD 2,000.00`, [`Exhibit 22`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_22_2018-06-04_Victim_Raul_Chat_1_Delivery_Delayed_9_Times_Account_Blocked_USD_2000.png)), **Matt** (`USD 1,844.00`, [`Exhibit 24`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_24_2018-11_Victim_Matt_Order_Summary_Jed_Board_AWD_USD_1545_plus_299_Deposit.png), [`Exhibit 25`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_25_2018-11-16_Victim_Matt_Jeremy_Bogan_Promise_Money_Will_Hit_Bank_in_Few_Days.png)), and **Charlie Chan** ([`Exhibit 45`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_45_2019-08_Victim_Charlie_Chan_Chat_Terminated_Credit_Card_Refund_Excuse.png), [`Exhibit 46`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_46_2019-08-21_Victim_Charlie_Chan_Singapore_Business_Directory_JED_BOARDS_PTE_LTD_28_Stanley_St.png)).

## 2. Written Admission of Unpaid Creditors — 1 November 2026 ([`Exhibit 71`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_71_2026-11-01_Jeremy_Bogan_Final_Response_Email_from_jb_at_opusfab_com_Refusing_Refund.eml))

Crucially, on **1 November 2026**, Jeremy Scott Bogan sent an email from his new corporate email address (`jb@opusfab.com`, [`Exhibit 71`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_71_2026-11-01_Jeremy_Bogan_Final_Response_Email_from_jb_at_opusfab_com_Refusing_Refund.eml)) explicitly acknowledging that Jed Boards had shut down with unpaid creditors and losses (*"When a company becomes insolvent and shuts down, people lose money — customers, suppliers, investors, and the founders themselves"*), while explicitly refusing repayment (*"I will not be paying you US$2,424 personally, now or in the future"*).

This admission raises serious questions about the accuracy of the company's declarations and representations made during the administrative striking-off process. An insolvent company with unpaid customer debts cannot lawfully declare zero liabilities to obtain an administrative Strike-Off under **Section 344** in order to bypass formal liquidation and examination of its **`SGD 400,000.00`** paid-up share capital.

## 4. Formal Requests for Regulatory Investigation

I respectfully request that ACRA:

1. **Investigate the striking-off application and statutory declarations:** Examine the striking-off application and related representations submitted for **`JED BOARDS PTE. LTD.` (`UEN: 201801893M`)** to determine whether potential offences occurred under:
   - **Section 401(2) of the Companies Act:** Making false or misleading statements to the Registrar regarding the absence of outstanding liabilities/creditors;
   - **Section 340 of the Companies Act:** Fraudulent trading.
2. **Examine the company's accounting records and financial transactions:** Investigate the accounting records and disposition of the **`SGD 400,000.00` in paid-up share capital**, together with customer pre-order funds received by `JED BOARDS PTE. LTD.` via `Pay2Day` ([`Exhibit 02`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_02_2018-06_ICS_Credit_Card_Statement_EUR_1705_28_Order_JED-1228-17.pdf), [`Exhibit 15`](https://joosthebly50.github.io/jed-boards-evidence-dossier/SCREENSHOTS/Exhibit_15_2019-08-19_ICS_Signed_Chargeback_Dispute_Form_Order_JED-1228-17.pdf)).
