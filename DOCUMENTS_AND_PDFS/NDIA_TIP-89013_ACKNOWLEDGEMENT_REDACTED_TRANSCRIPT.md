## Message body

Hello

Thank you for submitting your Tip-Off information to the NDIA.

Your reference number is: TIP-89013

We received your allegation via email. In future, we encourage you to submit any allegations through our user-friendly fraud reporting form on the NDIS website. The online form ensures we have as much information as possible to better assist the investigation process.

We take all reports seriously and they are assessed by specialist officers at the NDIA.

However, sometimes we can’t provide information on a matter because we need to protect the privacy of individuals or the integrity of an investigation.

We also may not be able to provide information about your matter(s) due to our obligations under the Privacy Act 1988

Fraud against the NDIS diverts funds away from participants and puts their safety and wellbeing at risk. The NDIA is committed to preventing, detecting, and responding to non-compliance and fraud by:

·       Providing education to correct claiming behaviour

·       Monitoring of all future claims

·       Changes to plan management

·       Undertaking fraud investigation

·       Referring evidence of fraud to the Office of the Commonwealth Director of Public Prosecutions for the prosecution of criminal offences

·       Removal or change of access to the scheme

If you wish to provide further information about a matter previously reported, please complete the online tip-off form with your reference number. You can also call the dedicated NDIS Fraud Reporting and Scams Helpline on 1800 650 717 and quote your reference number.

Regards

NDIS
